# Trigon Links — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [insert date]**

Trigon Links Communication ("Trigon Links", "we") operates a fibre broadband
network. This policy explains what the Trigon Links subscriber app collects, why,
and what we do with it.

## What the app collects

The app has no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. It
collects only what is needed to show you your own service:

- **Your account details** — name, user ID, mobile number, email, service address
  and national identity number (CNIC)
- **Your billing records** — invoices issued, amounts paid, outstanding balances,
  payment method and receipt numbers
- **Your support tickets** — the problem you describe, its priority and its status
- **Your connection details** — package, speed, PPPoE credentials and IP address

## How your CNIC is used

Your CNIC is stored to verify your identity at sign-in. It is visible to
authorised Trigon Links staff and is included in your bill and activation slip.

We keep it in two forms. The number itself is held on your subscriber record,
because it is the second factor that proves you are the account holder when you
sign in, and because staff need it to confirm your identity during installation,
billing and support. Alongside that record we also store a one-way salted hash of
the number, which is the value the sign-in check actually verifies against.

Access to your subscriber record is restricted. Only Trigon Links staff who are
assigned to your service area can retrieve it, and every staff action is recorded
in an audit log. Your CNIC is never used as a password, and it is never shown in
full inside the app: the app displays a masked form of it on your account screen.

## Who can see your data

- **You** see your own account, bills, payments and tickets.
- **Trigon Links staff** see the operational records needed to run the network,
  and only within the service areas they are assigned to.
- **Nobody else.** We do not sell, rent or share your data with advertisers,
  data brokers or third parties.

## Security

- All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. The app is configured to refuse
  unencrypted connections.
- Your session is held in a server-side cookie that JavaScript cannot read.
- The app requests no camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage access.
- Nothing in the app is included in device backups or transfer to a new phone.

## Retention

Billing records are retained for the period required by Pakistani tax and
telecom law. Support tickets are retained for service-quality reporting. You can
ask us to correct any inaccurate record at any time.

## Your rights

You may ask us to:

- confirm what we hold about you,
- correct anything that is wrong,
- delete your personal data where we are not legally required to keep it.

Requests go to the Trigon Links office or the helpline. We respond within the
period required by applicable law.

## Children

This app is for account holders. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
